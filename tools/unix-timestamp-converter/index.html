    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does a Unix timestamp represent?

    It counts time from 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC, commonly expressed in seconds or milliseconds.

    Why does a timestamp sometimes show the wrong date?

    Check whether the value uses seconds or milliseconds and whether the displayed time zone matches your expectation.

    Can I turn a UTC date into a Unix timestamp?

    Yes. Enter a UTC date and time using the tool's date-to-timestamp controls.

    Where are Unix timestamps commonly used?

    They appear in APIs, event logs, databases and other systems that record times.
