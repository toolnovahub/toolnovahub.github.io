    SVG to PNG

    Convert SVG vector images to PNG locally.

    Maximum SVG size: 2 MB. Maximum output: 40 megapixels. Transparency is preserved. External resources, scripts and embedded HTML are not supported. Files are processed on your device.
    QUICK GUIDE

    How to convert SVG to PNG

    Select an SVG file, choose the output resolution and click Convert to PNG. Your browser renders the vector artwork into a PNG image you can download. Transparent backgrounds remain transparent.

    Privacy first

    Conversion happens in your browser. No account or server upload is required.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why convert SVG to PNG?

    PNG is a widely supported raster format for apps and workflows that do not accept vector SVG files.

    Can I control the exported image resolution?

    Yes. Choose the desired output resolution before converting the vector artwork.

    Can the converted PNG preserve transparency?

    Yes. Transparent backgrounds in supported SVG artwork can remain transparent in the PNG.

    Why might an SVG file be rejected?

    For safety, the converter rejects unsupported or unsafe SVG content, including external references.
