    PRACTICAL GUIDE

    How to use Word Counter

    Measure words, characters and lines while editing a draft, essay, job description or website copy. Counts update as you type or paste text.

    1. Type or paste text into the editor.
    2. Check the updated word, character and line counts.
    3. Revise the text until it meets your target.

    How it works

    The browser examines the current text content and calculates common text metrics without transmitting the draft to a server.

    Real examples

    The short sentence Hello world! contains two words; use live updates to keep a text under a publishing limit.

    Essay length

    Track the word count of a draft.

    Publishing

    Fit a product description within a target length.

    Limitations and common mistakes

    • Word-count rules vary slightly between writing platforms.
    • Check both words and characters when submitting short text.
    • Word boundaries can be interpreted differently by office suites and publishing services.
    • Unicode punctuation, hyphenation and whitespace affect counts in different systems.
    • Assuming the tool's word count is identical to every editor's.
    • Checking only words when the destination has a character limit.
    Privacy first

    Processing happens in your browser. No account or server upload is required.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I count words in a document draft?

    Paste or type the text into the editor and review the updated word count.

    Does this tool also show characters and lines?

    Yes. The tool reports multiple text metrics, including characters and lines.

    Why might word counts differ between applications?

    Different editors may treat punctuation, hyphens or whitespace differently when counting words.

    Can I use the count for an essay or product description?

    Yes. Check the totals while editing to stay close to your required length.
