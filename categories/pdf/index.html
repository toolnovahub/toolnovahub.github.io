Which PDF tool should you use?

Combining or extracting pages

Choose Merge PDF when you want to combine two or more documents into one. Choose Split PDF to create a new PDF using selected pages or ranges. Both are designed to retain ordinary PDF page content.

Reducing a scanned PDF

Compress PDF works best with image-heavy files. It converts pages into JPEG images, meaning selectable text, links, forms, bookmarks, and accessibility tags will be lost. Keep your original. A compressed output is not guaranteed to be smaller or identical in visual quality.

PDFs and image files

Choose PDF to JPG to export pages as individual images or a ZIP. Choose JPG to PDF when you need JPG or PNG images collected into a document, one per page.

Privacy, compatibility and limits

These tools process your selected documents locally using libraries served by ToolsNova. Large PDFs can require substantial device memory, especially on mobile. Check the format, file-size and page-count limits shown in each tool. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.
