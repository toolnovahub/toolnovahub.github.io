Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Files and tool inputs

ToolsNova tools are designed to process your selected files and entered content locally in your browser. ToolsNova does not intentionally upload the files or content you process to its own servers. Standard website hosting still delivers the site files to your browser.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-E83MGYBPKF) to understand page visits, traffic sources, approximate geographic regions, device types, and popular tools. Google may process technical information such as online identifiers and browser information according to its policies. We do not intentionally send file contents or form inputs as analytics events.

Analytics scripts are not loaded until you select “Accept analytics.” If you reject, we do not load Google Analytics. Your preference is saved in your browser's local storage. You can change it using “Analytics preferences” at the bottom of any page. If you withdraw consent after accepting, the page reloads to stop future tracking; previously set Google cookies may remain until they expire or are cleared in your browser.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and third parties

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages. Some browsers or tools may use third-party resources as disclosed on their respective pages.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at our GitHub repository.
