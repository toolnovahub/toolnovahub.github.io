How to Compress a PDF Without Losing Quality

Learn when PDF compression helps, what it can change, and how to protect readable, searchable documents.

Why PDFs get large

A PDF can contain selectable text, vector drawings, fonts, images, and entire scanned pages. Scanned pages are often large because every page is an image. Reducing image resolution or JPEG quality can save space, but may soften fine details.

A text-only PDF may already be compact. Re-encoding its pages as images can actually increase its size, so a single compression approach does not suit every document.

What ToolsNova Compress PDF actually does

Our Compress PDF tool renders each PDF page to an image, compresses it as JPEG, and assembles a new PDF. It is suited to scanned or image-heavy PDFs, not documents where you must preserve their structure.

Important: selectable text, hyperlinks, forms, bookmarks, and accessibility tags are lost in the processed file. The phrase 'without losing quality' cannot be guaranteed for this method. Keep your original copy.

Choosing the right settings

Start with Balanced. If signatures or small type look blurry, try Higher image quality. If you prioritize a small attachment over sharp details, try Smaller file. Compare the actual result page by page instead of relying only on the percentage saved.

For example, a scanned set of receipts may tolerate stronger JPEG compression, while an electronically signed contract or accessible report should not be flattened.

A safe four-step workflow

Save a backup, check whether text is selectable, choose your setting, and review the downloaded result. The tool will not download a PDF when the result is larger than the original. It accepts up to 20 MB and 40 pages and processes the document locally in the browser.

For searchable PDFs, digital signatures, interactive forms, and accessibility-sensitive files, choose a tool designed to preserve PDF objects rather than rasterizing pages.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not repeatedly recompress the same scan; lossy artifacts can accumulate. Do not assume a PDF is private merely because it is small. And do not discard the original until every page and its contents have been checked.
